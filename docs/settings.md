# LinguaVox — Settings Guide

Open the LinguaVox popup by clicking the icon in your Chrome toolbar.

---

## Speech Language

The language you will speak in. Set to **Auto** to let LinguaVox detect it automatically, or pick a specific language for better accuracy with accents or dialects.

---

## Mode

| Mode | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| **Transcribe** | Converts your speech to text exactly as spoken |
| **Translate** | Converts your speech to a different language |
| **Enhance** | Transcribes and applies AI polish automatically |

---

## Output Language (Translation mode)

When Mode is set to **Translate**, choose the language you want the final text in.

Examples:
- Speak Ukrainian → output: professional English in Gmail
- Speak Spanish → output: German in Slack
- Speak any language → output: any of 21+ languages

---

## AI Enhancement

Improves your transcribed text automatically. Choose a style that fits your use case:

| Style | Best for |
|-------|---------|
| **Smart Polish** | Everyday use — fixes grammar, keeps your meaning |
| **Business Style** | Emails, reports, professional messages |
| **Grammar Only** | Spelling and grammar fix, no rephrasing |
| **Creative** | Social posts, storytelling, creative writing |
| **Casual** | Chat messages, informal conversation |
| **Academic** | Essays, research, formal documents |

Enhancement can be turned off — only Whisper transcription will run.

---

## Meeting Mode

Adds real-time translated subtitles to any video call (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, and others). Start it by right-clicking the LinguaVox icon in the Chrome toolbar. Choose the subtitle language before starting.

---

## Interface Language

Changes the language of the LinguaVox popup itself. Supports 14 languages. Does not affect transcription or output.

---

## Night Mode

Switches the popup to a dark theme.

---

## History

Recent transcriptions saved locally in your browser. Set how many to keep (10, 25, 50, or 100). History is private — stored only on your device, never on our servers.

---

## API Key

By default, LinguaVox uses a shared key pool (20 requests/day free). Add your own OpenAI API key for unlimited requests. Your key is stored securely in your browser and is only used for your own transcriptions.

To add your key: open [Dashboard → API Key](https://linguavox.uk/login)
